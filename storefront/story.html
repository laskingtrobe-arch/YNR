Our Story

Made, Not Printed
We don't fit in.
We're not trying to.

YnR started in Mpape, Abuja, with a brush, a blank tee, and no interest in looking like everything else on the rack. Four years later, that hasn't changed — every hoodie, tee, long sleeve and pair of jeans that leaves our hands is still painted piece by piece, not printed by the thousand.

We make for the people who'd rather own the one shirt nobody else has than the popular one everybody does. If you buy from YnR, you're not buying a design — you're buying the actual piece, painted once.
