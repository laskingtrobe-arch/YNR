Shipping & Returns

Last updated 21 September 2026
This is a draft. The returns stance below (final sale, with exceptions for defects or a wrong/damaged piece) is a reasonable default for hand-painted one-of-ones — not something we know is exactly your preference. Worth deciding deliberately rather than inheriting as-is.

The exact fee for your order is always confirmed with you — either shown at checkout for Abuja, or quoted on WhatsApp for everywhere else — before anything is charged.

Dispatch time

Because every piece is hand-painted or hand-finished, dispatch isn't instant the way a factory-made item would be. We'll confirm a realistic estimate with you when we confirm your order on WhatsApp.

Returns

Every piece is one-of-one — once it's painted, there isn't a second identical one to send as a replacement, which is different from how returns usually work for mass-produced clothing. Because of that:

  • Hand-painted pieces are final sale once shipped: we can't accept a return simply because of a change of mind.
  • If a piece arrives damaged in transit, is not what you ordered, or has a genuine fault beyond the natural brushwork variation described in our terms, message us within a few days of delivery with a photo. We'll sort it out — a repair, a repaint, or a refund, depending on what's actually wrong.
  • If a piece you wanted has already sold, you can ask us to repaint a new one for you — the product page for any sold piece has a "Request A Repaint" option.

Refunds

Where a refund is agreed, it's returned to the original payment method. Card refunds through Paystack can take a few business days to reflect, depending on your bank.

Questions

About a specific order, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference.
